嗜中性白血球參與發炎性疾病的機轉與角色

Mechanisms and Pathological Roles of Neutrophils in Inflammatory Diseases
返回主題導覽

Neutrophils are the most abundant leukocyte population in the circulation and play a critical role in immune surveillance through pathogen phagocytosis and the formation of neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs). While NETs are essential for controlling microbial infections, a variety of endogenous stimuli—including immune complexes, advanced glycation end products (AGEs), and damage-associated molecular pattern (DAMP) molecules—can also induce NET formation. Aberrant NET release triggered by these self-derived signals has been implicated in the pathogenesis of several inflammatory diseases, such as systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), rheumatoid arthritis, cardiovascular disorders, and age-related inflammation. These stimuli can disrupt mitochondrial homeostasis, leading to the release of mitochondrial reactive oxygen species (ROS) and mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA), which further propagate inflammatory responses and contribute to metabolic dysregulation and cellular dysfunction. Our research utilizes multiple experimental platforms to investigate how DAMP-induced metabolic reprogramming, mitochondrial dysregulation, and NET formation in neutrophils contribute to the development and progression of inflammatory diseases.

樹突狀細胞生物學在發展癌症疫苗及佐劑研發的關鍵角色

Dendritic Cell Biology in Cancer Vaccine Development and Novel Adjuvants
返回主題導覽

We are working on other projects focused on understanding how innate immune receptors harness host immunity. Specifically, we are investigating how the signaling cascade driven by CLEC5A regulates antigen cross-presentation through signaling cascade and the metabolic changes in dendritic cells. We hypothesize that CLEC5A signaling in dendritic cells plays a crucial role in shaping the clonality and diversity of T cell. Our other project involves studying the principles of γδ+ T activation and IL-17 production. We are investigating the molecular mechanisms by establishing an in vitro γδ+ T cell expansion platform, followed by driving γδ+ T cells activation using pure cytokines or pathogen-activated dendritic cells.

The protein-based nanoparticle (PNP) would be generated by a hydrophilic shell conjugated with the ligand of a specific innate immune receptor, CLEC5A, where the inner core is a hydrophobic antigen. This PNP can stimulate the CLEC5A receptor signaling cascade in dendritic cells (DCs) while simultaneously being taken up by them for DC maturation and antigen cross-presentation. Through this CLEC5A-dependent mechanism, mature DCs effectively prime naïve CD8+ T cells, resulting in the robust expansion of antigen-specific cytotoxic T cells.

In tumor-bearing mice, we hypothesized that vaccination with CLEC5A-engaging PNPs enhances cross-presentation in lymphoid tissues, augments CD8⁺ T-cell responses, and ultimately contributes to a measurable reduction in tumor burden. This work highlights the potential of receptor-targeted nanovaccine to influence T-cell clonality and functional diversity by integrating innate receptor signaling with precise antigen delivery.

先天免疫檢查點 NLRP12

NLRP12 as a Critical Innate Immune Checkpoint and Homeostasis Regulator
返回主題導覽

NLRP12 regulates signaling cascades activated by Toll-like receptors, RIG-I like receptor and cGAS-STING pathway, impacting the production of proinflammatory cytokines and type I IFN. This led us to name NLRP12 an innate immune checkpoint (Cell Host & Microbes, 2019; Journal of Clinical Investigation, 2023). Recently, we have been investigating the role of NLRP12 in the pathogenesis of several inflammatory diseases, including SLE, sepsis, arthritis and vascular stiffness, which are linked to dysregulated NET formation and type I IFN signatures.

結合臨床醫學所需的免疫檢測平台

Translational Immune Diagnostic Platforms for Clinical Medicine
返回主題導覽

We established a platform to validate the accuracy of the T cell recognition peptides, typically derived from algorithms. In fact, outcomes of peptide prediction are not always compatible with real-world immune responses. This discrepancy arises because peptide prediction algorithms are primarily based on HLA structure and the peptide-HLA, often overlooking the natural process of antigen cross-presentation in dendritic cells. Therefore, this in vitro platform helps bridge this gap by enabling antigen presentation using actual proteins. This allows dendritic cells to activate T cells with the natural selected peptides within the context of antigen presentation, rather than relying solely on peptides selected by artificial algorithms. Our tool offers a physiologically relevant method for validating candidate peptides, enabling the analysis of epitope-specific T cell immune responses. The dominant T cell clones generated by this platform are also valuable for diagnosis and their application in cancer immunotherapy.

紅斑性狼瘡的致病機轉與治療

Pathogenesis and Targeted Therapeutic Strategies for Systemic Lupus Erythematosus
返回主題導覽

經過半世紀的努力，紅斑性狼瘡已非絕症，狼瘡病人的存活已大幅改善。目前多數的藥物已能夠穩定的控制病人的疾病活動度，然而針對紅斑性狼瘡病人進展成棘手的狼瘡性腎炎以及神經性症狀給予有效且低副作用的藥物控制仍是醫學上未滿足的需求。由於紅斑性狼瘡無法完全治癒，只能透過藥物及良好的生活及飲食習慣控制病情，因此病人須終身使用藥物。以往用於治療器官侵犯如狼瘡腎炎的小分子藥物以及單株抗體生物製劑，以靶向後天免疫系統活化亦即T與B淋巴球的活化為主。由於生物製劑價格昂貴，因此多數病人在沒有保險及健保補助下，仍以小分子藥物緩步降低自身免疫活化的情況，這些藥物雖然是免疫調節劑，但仍然屬於廣效的免疫抑制劑，長期服用後會出現感染及惡性腫瘤等嚴重的副作用。 免疫學的進展讓科學家更了解紅斑性狼瘡的致病機轉，現今的研究趨勢指出先天免疫活化的路徑是紅斑性狼瘡病患免疫活化的起點，該路徑也在致病機轉扮演吃重的角色。目前除了已有新核准的莎芙諾®注射劑用來抑制第一型干擾素受體訊號，但該藥屬於藥價高昂的生物製劑，若能開發用以阻斷先天免疫系統活化途徑的可口服之小分子標的藥物，例如胞內核酸受體cGAS、阻斷粒線體氧化壓力、以及阻斷中性球特異性死亡等途徑，可望從源頭阻絕免疫系統活化，為穩定病患疾病活性提出更為經濟的新解方。
